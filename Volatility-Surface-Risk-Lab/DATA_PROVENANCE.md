# Browser data provenance

The public page contains **no third-party market observations**. Its date interval indexes a deterministic, project-authored synthetic regime formula. Surface values, shocks, scenarios, P&L, VaR, and Expected Shortfall are generated in the browser from visible controls and a seed.

The dates are teaching coordinates, not measured SPX, VIX, Cboe, WRDS, or OptionMetrics history. The public browser is not a substitute for a database subscription and does not expose the licensed 2005–2021 research sample.

Detailed empirical results remain withheld pending written confirmation under Bocconi University's institutional agreements.
