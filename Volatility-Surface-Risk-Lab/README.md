# Volatility Surface Risk Lab

A public-safe teaching laboratory for volatility-surface representation, Parametric Surface Projection, seeded scenarios, and option-portfolio VaR and Expected Shortfall.

## Thesis origin and extension

This project began as **Alireza Moslemi Haghighi's master's-thesis work on Parametric Surface Projection (PSP)**, developed with Shiva Zamani and Hamid Arian and documented in Zamani, Moslemi Haghighi, and Arian (2023), arXiv:2311.14985. It was subsequently extended into a 2005–2021 research platform with PCA, Heston models, nonlinear fixed-strike repricing, and formal validation.

## Public-safe design

The browser generates all period inputs and scenarios locally from transparent formulas and a visible seed. It contains no WRDS, OptionMetrics, Cboe, exchange, or other vendor observations. The year selector changes synthetic regime parameters and teaching risk outputs; it does not filter licensed thesis data.

Detailed vendor-data-derived tables and the extended empirical report are withheld pending written institutional confirmation.
