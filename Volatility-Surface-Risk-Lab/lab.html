01

Surface

Fit is not forecast.

B-splines and Heston can represent the same observed option market differently. Held-out quote error evaluates representation; VaR backtests evaluate transition dynamics.

02

Measure

P and Q have different jobs.

Risk-neutral Heston parameters price contingent claims. A VaR engine needs real-world scenario probabilities, so Heston-MC-P estimates a separate trailing physical transition.

03

Validation

One passing level is not enough.

Heston-MC-P passes joint conditional coverage at 95% in the study, but not at 90% or 99%. Tail performance and exception clustering remain model risks.
